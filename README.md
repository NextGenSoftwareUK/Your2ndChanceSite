# Your 2nd Chance — We See You

**OASIS Omniverse · For People Who Need a Fresh Start**

Your 2nd Chance is an OASIS Omniverse platform for people who need — and deserve — a second chance. Whether you're leaving prison, recovering from addiction, escaping homelessness, fleeing abuse, or simply starting over after life fell apart: **we see you, and you are welcome here.**

## The Mission

The criminal justice system brands people. Addiction takes everything. Homelessness isolates. Trauma silences. Your 2nd Chance exists to say: none of that defines you. What you do next does.

This platform connects people rebuilding their lives with:
- Practical resources (housing, employment, legal aid, benefits)
- Peer support from people who've been through it
- Skill-building pathways integrated with [Justice League Academy](../Justice-League-Academy)
- A karma system that rewards every positive step — however small

## Features

- **Resource finder** — housing, employment, addiction support, legal help, mental health services
- **Peer mentorship** — connect with people further along their own second-chance journey
- **Skill pathways** — integrated with JLA for free education and skill-building
- **Progress tracking** — visual milestones that celebrate every step forward
- **Karma integration** — every positive action earns OASIS karma; a record of growth, not shame
- **Anonymous mode** — use the platform without revealing your identity

## OASIS Integration

Every step taken on Your 2nd Chance earns OASIS karma — creating a positive digital record that carries across the entire OASIS Omniverse. For people whose offline record follows them negatively, this offers something genuinely new: a ledger of *who you are becoming*, not just who you were.
